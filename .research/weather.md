# Lilly Miller Moss Out! Lawn Moss Killer Concentrate — Application Weather Guide

## Recommended Weather Conditions

| Condition | Recommendation |
|---|---|
| **Rain** | No rain expected for at least **24 hours** after application |
| **Temperature** | Warm weather, above **50°F (10°C)** — works fastest in warm, dry conditions |
| **Frost** | Avoid applying when ground is frozen or freezing temps are forecast |
| **Time of day** | Morning or late afternoon (after dew is gone, before peak heat) |

## Other Tips

- Use **plastic** sprinklers/sprayers only — the product corrodes metal
- Mow shorter and rake out thick moss **before** applying to improve coverage
- Don't water in; let it sit on the moss
- Rake out dead (blackened) moss after a few days
- Avoid spraying on walkways/patios — it can stain
